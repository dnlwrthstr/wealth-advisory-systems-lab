# Fitch Ratings

Fitch Ratings provides credit ratings for a variety of entities and financial instruments.

## Rating Scale

Fitch Ratings' credit ratings are expressed as letter grades that range from 'AAA' to 'D'.

### Investment Grade
- **AAA**: Highest credit quality. Denotes the lowest expectation of default risk.
- **AA+, AA, AA-**: Very high credit quality.
- **A+, A, A-**: High credit quality.
- **BBB+, BBB, BBB-**: Good credit quality.

### Speculative Grade
- **BB+, BB, BB-**: Speculative.
- **B+, B, B-**: Highly speculative.
- **CCC+, CCC, CCC-**: Substantial credit risk.
- **CC**: Very high levels of credit risk.
- **C**: Near default.
- **RD**: Restricted default.
- **D**: Default.
